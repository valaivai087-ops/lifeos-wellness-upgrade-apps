② 高衝突循環 | 情緒互相污染

② High Conflict Cycles | Emotional Contamination
Direct emotional reactions, lack of pause and observation, amplifying minor issues, mutual defensiveness, repeated conflicts, emotional blackmail, high-alert interactions, rational system offline
③ 投射型關係 | 把內在問題丟給別人
把情緒怪罪對方、把創傷投射別人、過度讀心術、差異視為敵意、把過去帶進現在、情緒=真相
③ Projection-Based Relationships | Externalizing Inner Problems
Blaming others for emotions, projecting trauma, mind-reading, seeing differences as hostility, bringing the past into present, emotions as truth
④ 討好人格 | 失去真實自己

Unable to refuse, always accommodating, fear of others' anger, suppressed needs, collapsed boundaries, chronic resentment, loss of self

三層的決策路徑 | Decision Paths at Three Levels

🔴 生存者：
刺激 → 反應
（無中介層，直接情緒反應）

🟡 觀察者：
刺激 → 模型 → 理解
（有認知中介層）

🟢 創造者：
模型 → 行動 → 生成
（反向生成，主動創造）
🔴 Survivor:
Stimulus → Reaction
(No intermediary, direct emotional response)

🟡 Observer:
Stimulus → Model → Understanding
(Cognitive intermediary)

🟢 Creator:
Model → Action → Generation
(Reverse generation, active creation)

升維路徑 | Upgrade Pathways

從生存者到觀察者：
需要學會停頓、觀察、反思。在刺激和反應之間，加入思考的空間。

從觀察者到創造者：
需要從分析進入生成。不只看懂世界，還要主動創造條件、塑造現實。
From Survivor to Observer:
Learn to pause, observe, and reflect. Insert thinking space between stimulus and response.

From Observer to Creator:
Move from analysis to generation. Not just understanding the world, but actively creating conditions and shaping reality.
