🔑 核心發現 | Core Discovery ▼
中文
語言不是描述現實的工具，語言本身就是構建現實的材料。你說什麼，大腦就預測什麼；大腦預測什麼，行動就跟著什麼；行動累積什麼，人生就變成什麼。
English
Language is not a tool to describe reality; language itself is the material that constructs reality. What you say, your brain predicts; what your brain predicts, your actions follow; what your actions accumulate, your life becomes.
🧠 四大控制模組 | Four Core Modules ▼
中文
👁 感知模組
語言決定你看見機會或風險。限制語言啟動風險過濾，成長語言啟動探索模式。
🤝 資源模組
語言決定他人如何回應你。低價值語言被忽略，價值語言被靠近。
🪞 身份模組
語言定義你的自我模型。自我否定導向放棄，自我賦能導向嘗試。
⚡ 行動模組
語言直接觸發行動。拖延語言停滯，行動語言推進。
English
👁 Perception Module
Language determines whether you see opportunity or risk. Limiting language activates risk filters; growth language activates exploration mode.
🤝 Resource Module
Language determines how others respond to you. Low-value language gets overlooked; value-rich language attracts people.
🪞 Identity Module
Language defines your self-model. Self-denial leads to giving up; self-empowerment leads to attempting.
⚡ Action Module
Language directly triggers action. Procrastination language stalls; action language propels forward.
🔄 14種語言重構模式 | 14 Reframing Patterns
1️⃣ 意圖重構 | Intention Reframe ▼
中文
本體： 行為是表面，意圖是核心。解讀底層意圖比反應行為更有效。
機制： 錯把行為當惡意 → 情緒爆炸 → 衝突擴大。看見意圖 → 降低誤解 → 關係改善。
例句：
❌ 低維
他在批評我
✅ 高維
他在指出可以優化的地方
English
Ontology: Behavior is surface, intention is core. Decoding underlying intentions is more effective than reacting to behavior.
Mechanism: Mistaking behavior for malice → Emotional explosion → Conflict escalates. Seeing intention → Reducing misunderstanding → Relationship improves.
Example:
❌ Low-Dimensional
He is criticizing me.
✅ High-Dimensional
He is pointing out areas for optimization.
2️⃣ 重新定義 | Redefinition ▼
中文
本體： 失敗不是終點，是資料。標籤會鎖死你，定義會解放你。
機制： 標籤思維→卡死。資料思維→可更新。
例句：
❌ 低維
我失敗了
✅ 高維
我獲得了一組無效策略的反饋
English
Ontology: Failure is not an endpoint, it's data. Labels lock you down; definitions set you free.
Mechanism: Label thinking → Stuck. Data thinking → Updatable.
Example:
❌ Low-Dimensional
I failed.
✅ High-Dimensional
I received feedback on an ineffective strategy set.
3️⃣ 結果框架 | Outcome Frame ▼
中文
本體： 你看哪裡，就走哪裡。注意力決定方向。
機制： 問題框架→困在問題。結果框架→指向解答。
例句：
❌ 低維
如何避免失敗？
✅ 高維
我要達到什麼樣的成功？
English
Ontology: Where you look is where you go. Attention determines direction.
Mechanism: Problem frame → Stuck in problem. Outcome frame → Points to solution.
Example:
❌ Low-Dimensional
How do I avoid failure?
✅ High-Dimensional
What kind of success do I want to achieve?
4️⃣ 因果重構 | Causality Reframe ▼
中文
本體： 掌控感從內參與開始。你對自己的選擇有多少認知。
機制： 外歸因→無力感→停滯。內參與→掌控感→行動。
例句：
❌ 低維
都是他害的
✅ 高維
我要重新建立邊界
English
Ontology: Control begins with internal participation. Your sense of agency starts from awareness of choice.
Mechanism: External attribution → Powerlessness → Stagnation. Internal participation → Control → Action.
Example:
❌ Low-Dimensional
It's all his fault.
✅ High-Dimensional
I need to re-establish clear boundaries.
5️⃣ 時間框架 | Time Frame ▼
中文
本體： 當下只是切片，不是全部。時間視野決定判斷。
機制： 短期絕望→長期機會。擴展時間視野→發現模式。
例句：
❌ 低維
人生毀了
✅ 高維
這是長期成長中的一個重要切片
English
Ontology: Now is just a slice, not the whole. Time perspective determines judgment.
Mechanism: Short-term despair → Long-term opportunity. Expanding time horizon → Discovering patterns.
Example:
❌ Low-Dimensional
My life is ruined.
✅ High-Dimensional
This is one important slice in my long-term growth journey.

30天語言升級挑戰 | 30-Day Language Upgrade Challenge ▼
中文
第1-10天：識別階段
每天記錄你說的5句低維語言。只記錄，不評判。目的是提高覺察。

第11-20天：轉化階段
每天選擇最常說的低維語言，查找本APP中的對應高維表達。大聲說3遍新語言。

第21-30天：鞏固階段
在生活中主動使用高維語言。記錄每次使用時的感受變化。
English
Days 1-10: Awareness Phase
Record 5 low-dimensional sentences you say each day. Just observe, don't judge. The goal is to increase awareness.

Days 11-20: Transformation Phase
Choose the most frequently-used low-dimensional language, find the corresponding high-dimensional expression in this APP, and say the new language aloud 3 times.

Days 21-30: Consolidation Phase
Actively use high-dimensional language in life. Record how your feelings shift each time you use it.
