第 1 課
十架總論
The Doctrine of the Cross
🧠 本課心智圖框架
十字架 救贖 信仰中心 神的愛 永恆生命
今天真的很喜樂，因著眾弟兄姐妹的渴慕，借著大家同心合一的禱告，主給了我們這個聚會。
Today I am truly joyful, because of the eager hearts of all the brothers and sisters, and through our unified prayers together, the Lord has granted us this gathering.
近幾年來，一些新的弟兄姐妹來到我們教會當中，他們對福音基礎信息、完整而全備的福音系統還不太瞭解。
In recent years, some new brothers and sisters have come to our church, and they don't yet fully understand the basic gospel message and the complete gospel system.
所以我們要重講一次《福音36課》。神的道在其中，每一個弟兄姐妹都會有所裨益的。
Therefore we need to teach these Gospel 36 Lessons again. God's Word is in it, and every brother and sister will benefit greatly from it.
預備講36課的時候呢，我有兩個挑戰。第一個是絕對不能讓這信息成為一個知識。
When preparing to teach these 36 lessons, I faced two challenges. The first is to absolutely not let this message become mere knowledge.
當福音信息淪為一個知識的時候，各位聽了一課、兩課、三課，很快就會麻木的。
When the gospel message becomes mere knowledge, after listening to one, two, three lessons, you will quickly become numb to it.

第 2 課
唯一的問題
The Only Question
🧠 本課心智圖框架
人生問題 靈魂救贖 罪的根源 永恆關係 意義尋求
今天的主題是《唯一的問題》，大家要有跟問題征戰的心。
Today's topic is "The Only Question," and we must have hearts ready to do battle against problems.
在聚會時，如果有受干擾、不能集中的狀況，請向神禱告。
During this gathering, if you experience disturbances or cannot concentrate, please pray to God.
倘若禱告卻仍然受影響，請不要擔憂，不要懼怕，因為在聚會中你正在經歷靈戰。
If you still feel affected despite praying, do not worry or fear, for you are experiencing spiritual warfare in this gathering.
每一次聚會裡，前面的話是非常重要的，各位若聽得懂，後面所講的便會全部掌握。
In every gathering, the opening words are extremely important. If you understand them, you will grasp everything that follows.
首先，各位要明白什麼是「問題」，這很重要。
First, you must understand what a "problem" is. This is very important.
